On the only bid with a full document pair (Northside), the new process:
- Confirmed all 8 hard rules pass.
- Caught a real GP/client accounting inconsistency (G&A absorbed vs subtracted) that has no test in the existing skills.
- Re-surfaced the GP report's own tonnage exposure flag (~20-25T over-quote risk) and tied it back to the dollar impact.
- Flagged the missing HISD prequalification statement.

On the other two bids, the process correctly failed at the artifact gate: it cannot validate a $485K or $625K estimated value without a corresponding versioned proposal PDF in output/. That gap is itself a finding - the Virtual Office is recording estimated_value into bid_pipeline.db without persisting the two-PDF pair that the rules require.

## Recommended actions

1. **Northside**: resolve the GP G&A double-count and the tonnage check before final sign-off. Add HISD prequal qualification line to the client proposal.
2. **Kinder Morgan and Marathon**: locate the source proposals. If they exist outside output/, mirror them into output/ so the validator has something to check. If they don't exist, generate them before any further status change.
3. **System-level**: tighten the bid_pipeline.db state machine so a bid cannot move to SUBMITTED without a corresponding output/ PDF pair. That single gate would have caught Marathon and Kinder Morgan.
