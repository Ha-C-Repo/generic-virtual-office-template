## WHAT A BRAND FILM IS (vs. a commercial)

A commercial sells a product or drives a specific action.
A brand film sells a belief — why the company exists and what it stands for.

Brand films are felt, not just watched. They do not pitch. They earn trust
by showing the world through the brand's lens. Viewers should finish one
and feel something — then remember who made them feel it.

**The rule:** Show, don't tell. If you're saying "we're the best," you're
making a commercial. If the viewer is thinking "these people get it," you're
making a brand film.

## STRUCTURE — 60-SECOND BRAND FILM (10-12 clips × 5s)

| Time | Beat | Narrative Function | Shot Type | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:05 | OPENING IMAGE | World — establishes the universe | Atmospheric wide or ECU detail | Ambient sound, no VO |
| 0:05-0:12 | THE WORLD | How things are — the context | 2 clips: environment + human | Quiet music begins |
| 0:12-0:22 | THE TENSION | What's broken, missing, or hard | 2 clips: contrast or struggle | VO begins — low, deliberate |
| 0:22-0:35 | THE TURN | The belief that changes things | 2 clips: action, craft, insight | VO builds — the core idea |
| 0:35-0:48 | THE PROOF | What that belief looks like in reality | 2-3 clips: work, result, people | VO rises — evidence |
| 0:48-0:55 | THE FEELING | The emotional payoff — aspiration | 1 clip: human moment or beauty shot | VO resolves — lands the idea |
| 0:55-1:00 | BRAND CLOSE | Company name + tagline | Logo card or final image | Music out / VO out |

## SCRIPT FORMAT — BRAND FILM VO

```
[PROJECT NAME] — 60s Brand Film
VO word count: 90-110 words (slower delivery, intentional pauses)
Voice direction: [calm authority / warm and grounded / weathered and real]

OPENING (0:00-0:12) — NO VO
VISUAL: [atmospheric — world-building]
AUDIO: [ambient sound — shop noise, wind, city, etc.]

TENSION (0:12-0:22)
VO: "[Problem or reality — 20-25 words. Spoken like an observation, not a sales pitch.]"

THE TURN (0:22-0:35)
VO: "[The belief — 25-30 words. This is the brand's point of view on how things should be.]"

THE PROOF (0:35-0:48)
VO: "[What this looks like in practice — 25-30 words. Specific, not generic.]"

CLOSE (0:48-1:00)
VO: "[The landing — 15-20 words. A statement, not a slogan. Then company name.]"
ON-SCREEN: [Company name] | [Tagline]
AUDIO: [Music resolves]
```

## BRAND FILM SHOT DESIGN

**The rule for each clip:** Every shot should be able to stand alone as
a photograph. If you had to pick 10 frames and put them in a gallery,
would each one hold up on its own?

**Shot variety for visual rhythm:**
- Alternate between intimate (ECU/CU) and expansive (WS/EWS) — never
  run 3 wide shots or 3 close shots in a row
- One "hero" shot per thematic section — the image the viewer remembers
- At least one detail shot that reveals craft or care (not just people talking)

**People:** Caught in the act of doing something real — not posed.
The most powerful brand film shots show expertise without narration.

## RUNWAY WORKFLOW PLAN — 60s BRAND FILM

```
PHASE 1 — ENVIRONMENT REFERENCES (generate these first)
  Primary environment reference → Gen-4 Image
  Secondary environment reference → Gen-4 Image

PHASE 2 — VIDEO GENERATION (10 clips, 5s each)

Section A — World (Clips 01-02, atmospheric, no characters)
  Text [Clip 01: opening image] → Gen-4.5 Video 16:9
  Text [Clip 02: environment detail] → Gen-4.5 Turbo 16:9

Section B — Tension (Clips 03-04)
  Text [Clip 03: human context] → Gen-4.5 Turbo (first frame = env ref)
  Text [Clip 04: contrast or difficulty] → Gen-4.5 Turbo (first frame = Last Frame 03)

Section C — The Turn (Clips 05-06)
  Text [Clip 05: action or decision] → Gen-4.5 Turbo (first frame = env ref)
  Text [Clip 06: craft or care shown] → Gen-4.5 Turbo (first frame = Last Frame 05)

Section D — Proof (Clips 07-09)
  Text [Clip 07: result or outcome] → Gen-4.5 Turbo
  Text [Clip 08: human moment] → Gen-4.5 Turbo
  Text [Clip 09: beauty detail] → Gen-4.5 Turbo

Section E — Brand Close (Clip 10)
  Text [Clip 10: logo card / final image] → Gen-4.5 Video (minimal motion)

PHASE 3 — AUDIO
  Text [VO script, ~100 words] → Text to Speech
  Text [Score: "slow-building cinematic underscore, minimal, resolves at 55s"] → Text to SFX
  Text [Ambient layer: "[environment ambience]"] → Text to SFX

PHASE 4 — ASSEMBLY
  Clips 01-10 → Stitch
  Stitch + TTS + Score + Ambient → Add Audio (layered)
  → Upscale 4K
```

**Credit Estimate:**
- 10 clips × Turbo (25) = 250
- 2 reference images = 20
- Audio (TTS + 2 SFX) = 25
- 4K upscale (60s × 2cr/s) = 120
- **Draft total: ~415 credits**
- **Final quality (Gen-4.5 full): ~820 credits**

## BRAND FILM QA — ADDITIONAL CHECKS

Beyond the standard QA checklist, brand films require:

- [ ] VO delivers the brand's core belief — not just product features
- [ ] No clip feels like it belongs in a different film
- [ ] The opening image is strong enough to hold silence for 5 seconds
- [ ] The close is a statement, not a slogan
- [ ] Shot variety — no 3 consecutive shots of the same type
- [ ] The brand name appears ONCE in VO and ONCE on screen — not more
- [ ] Ambient audio layer is present — the world has sound
