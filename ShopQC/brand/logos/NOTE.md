# Bundled logo masters (Tier 1)

These two files are byte-identical copies of the approved Your Company masters in
the repo-root `brand/logos/`, bundled here so the Shop QC EXE is self-contained and
`config.resource_path("brand/logos/...")` resolves them in both dev and the frozen
build. They are reproduced unchanged. Do not edit, recolor, recreate, or replace
them; update only from the canonical masters. See `brand/LOGO_RULES.md`.

- `Your Company LLC.png` - silver mark on dark, used on the dark (#141414) PDF
  header band.
- `your company.png` - black mark on transparent, for any future light-background
  placement.
