Staff Training Suite

The full operations curriculum, learning from the front desk up. It runs from foundation and daily tools through operational discipline and business domain, into compliance and projects, then into the executive-assistant and office-manager work that keeps all three companies running. Every module is interactive and self-paced, built around how Your Company actually operates.

How to use

Click any module to open it. Lessons unlock sequentially within each module (Lesson 2 requires Lesson 1 complete, etc.), but the suite itself is unlocked. Take them in order for the build-up; jump around if you need a specific topic. Progress is in-memory per module session.

About this suite

Built specifically for Your Company's front-office training. Every example, name, vendor reference, and dollar figure comes from how the company actually operates. No fictionalized scenarios.

Source of truth for compliance facts (ISN ID, Texas Mutual policy, Marathon pathway steps): the active operational documents and Paul's, Amber's, and the Owner's direct input.

If something looks wrong, it probably is. Flag it to Joseph in Teams (Admin-Ops channel) and the module gets corrected.
