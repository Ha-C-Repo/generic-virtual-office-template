Shop QC Software

The Shop QC app is how Your Company proves a piece of steel was received, fabricated, inspected, and released the right way. It runs as five tabs left to right: Projects, Receiving (Gate 1), Fabrication (Gate 2), Release (Gate 3), and the NCR Log. The gates sign in order and the hard blocks below cannot be skipped.

The three gates, in order

Every job moves through three sign-off gates from left to right. You cannot jump ahead.

Gate 1 · ReceivingGate 2 · FabricationGate 3 · Release

Gate 1 logs incoming material. Gate 2 runs the in-process traveler on the shop floor. Gate 3 is final release and shipping. The NCR Log (Tab 5) tracks any nonconformance across the whole lifecycle.

Hard block

Gate 2: the locked floor-step sequence

On the Fabrication traveler the floor steps are signed in a locked sequence. The app only lets you sign the lowest-numbered unsigned floor step. You cannot sign out of order or sign several at once. Finish the current step before the next one opens.

Hard block

Field 8: the CWI gate

Field 8 is the pre-weld inspection step. The traveler will not advance past it until a Certified Welding Inspector (CWI) name is entered. No name, no signature, no advance. This protects the weld record.

Hard block

Gate 3: the CEO co-sign

Any project of 50 tons or more, or any IAS job, requires the CEO co-sign at Release. The CEO name field must read exactly The Owner. Anything else is blocked. Initials, a different officer, or a misspelling will not pass.

The NCR lifecycle

When something is out of spec you raise a Nonconformance Report (NCR) against the job. The NCR is worked to resolution and then closed on the NCR Log tab. That is the one place an NCR is closed, so the record stays complete.
