## Are You Properly Specifying Materials?
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBE1c_R9gQ4 | Duration: 1:01:33 | Upload: NA | Tier: 1 | Watched: 2026-06-24
### Core Concept
Larry Kruth (AISC VP of Engineering and Research, former VP at Douglas Steel Fabricating) teaches how to specify the correct, current ASTM material designation for each structural shape and fastener, and the preferred (usually most economical) grade [01:04][01:34]. The controlling lesson is to avoid over-specifying, which he frames as a square peg chipped to fit a round hole where every chip costs money, and under-specifying, a round peg in a square hole that leaves the design short of its required properties [06:17][07:19][07:49]. The webinar is built on Jonathan Tavares' April 2018 Modern Steel Construction article, part of a series AISC updates on a 3-year cycle because material designations change [01:34][03:10].

### Technical Details
W shapes are ASTM A992 (always 50 ksi, no grade suffix); A588 gives atmospheric corrosion resistance at 50 ksi yield and 70 ksi tensile but costs more; A913 serves very large shapes in grades 50, 65, and 70 [13:31][14:03][14:34]. HP shapes moved from A36 to A572 Grade 50 in 2016 [10:25][22:54]. Rectangular HSS is A500 Grade C at 50 ksi (replacing the old Grade B at 46 ksi); round HSS at A500 Grade C is 46 ksi; A1085 lets you use the true wall thickness instead of A500's 0.93 design factor, caps yield at 70 ksi, and adds a 25 ft-lb CVN at 40 degrees F [23:25][25:05][26:06]. Steel pipe is A53 Grade B at 35 ksi and is not the same as round HSS [28:41][29:11]. Plates and bars are A36 (to 8 in, dropping to 32 ksi above) or A572 Grade 50 (to 4 in) [31:27][32:31].
### Fabrication Notes
WT and ST tees are not rolled at mills; they are W and S shapes split down the middle in the fabricator's shop, so they inherit the parent shape's material spec [22:23]. A913 grades 50 and 65 do not require weld preheat, a real shop saving since preheating a very heavy section in cold weather (around 30 to 32 degrees F) can take up to 24 hours to reach welding temperature; grade 70 still needs preheat [16:37][17:07]. Over-specifying drives shop cost: a C12x20.7 will work as a stair stringer but the MC12x14.3 (with its 2-1/8 in flange that lets a handrail post weld flat without crimping) buys less material for the same job [19:46][20:18]. A53 pipe carries an added hydrostatic test cost that a structural round HSS avoids [30:53].
### Erection Notes
Anchor rod field issues dominate the erection content. Hole diameters in Manual Table 14-2 are oversized and coordinated with ACI 117 placement tolerances, and washers are sized to cover the hole and thick enough to develop the required strength; washers may be square or round [41:18][41:49]. Kruth stresses specifying F1554 Grade 55 with supplement S1 (weldable) so the field crew can weld a threaded extension or tie to the base plate when a rod is set too low or out of projection [40:48][57:56]. Grade 105 is high strength but never weldable, so a low-set Grade 105 rod cannot be field-corrected by welding [40:48][58:59].
### Common Errors / Inspection Failures
The recurring error is specifying outdated designations: A500 Grade B instead of Grade C, A36 HP shapes, or A992 Grade 50 (the grade suffix is wrong) [13:31][23:25]. Over-specifying (Charpy V-notch on all material, 70 ksi where 50 works, atmospheric corrosion steel where not needed) passes inspection but wastes money [06:48]. Confusing steel pipe (A53 Grade B, 35 ksi) with round HSS (A500 Grade C, 46 ksi) misstates strength [29:11]. Specifying F1554 Grade 55 without S1, or assuming Grade 105 can be welded, sets up a field failure when a rod needs correction [57:56][58:59].
### Bid / Cost Implications
Correct specification is a direct cost lever: A992 is cheaper than A913, A588 carries a premium over A992, A1085 carries a premium over A500, and the F3125 super-high-strength bolts (F3111, F3043) are unavailable in the US [16:06][26:06][31:59][35:04]. Kruth closes that proper specification eliminates RFIs and the substitution questions ("can I use this instead?") that consume estimator and PM time [48:05]. The 16th edition Manual rebases all connection-material tables from 36 ksi to 50 ksi, so connection takeoffs priced off the 15th edition assume the wrong default grade [45:29][46:00].
