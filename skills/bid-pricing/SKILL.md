## Additional Parameters
- G&A: 7.5% on direct cost
- Overhead multiplier: 1.15x
- Target net margin: ~25% after G&A
- Erection baseline: $8/SF commercial, field-adjusted

## Drawing-Stage Adders
- IFC: 0% (±5% qty tolerance only)
- DD: +3-5% (use +5% if bay or elevation missing)
- Budget/SD: +5-8% (use +8% for single-page or no EOR)
Adder on QUANTITY, never on price. Never disclosed to client.

## Small Project Override
Projects under $200K total: apply 50% profit across all line items.
Flag on GP report cover. No client disclosure.

## Payment Structure (30/20/50 LOCKED)
- 30% mobilization upon shop drawing approval (the "Trigger")
- 20% upon first delivery
- 50% per SOV (AIA G702/G703) through completion

## Cash Flow Validation (MANDATORY)
Before finalizing any bid:
1. 30% mobilization must cover all Phase 1 raw steel POs
2. 30% + 20% cumulative must cover all material costs
3. If not covered, flag on GP report - do not adjust payment terms
