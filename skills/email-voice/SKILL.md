# Email Voice (Owner's Rules)

## Core principles
- Short sentences. Specific numbers. No filler.
- No em-dashes (they signal AI). Use periods or hyphens.
- No "Great question!" or similar openers.
- No three-adjective lists.
- No "it's not just X, it's Y" constructions.
- Casual, human, structurally oriented.
- One ask per email. Not two. Not three.

## Signature
- Client emails: "Owner Steel" (not The Owner)
- Legal/contract: "The Owner"
- Always include title and contact block

## Structure
- Subject line: specific, no clickbait
- Opening: straight to the point
- Body: 2-4 short paragraphs max
- Close: one clear next action
- No apology paragraphs
- No corporate buzzwords (leverage, synergy, etc.)

## Copy-paste ready
Output must be plain text, ready to paste into Outlook/Gmail.
No markdown formatting. No bullet points unless specifically requested.
No HTML. Just clean text.

## Voice check before sending
Ask: "Would Owner actually say this out loud to a GC on a job site?"
If no, rewrite.
