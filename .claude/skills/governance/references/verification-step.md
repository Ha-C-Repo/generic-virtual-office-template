# Verification step (every high-risk skill)
Verify, do not generate, on anything that costs money if wrong.

- Cross-check the output against the input that produced it. reconciliation-check is the model: compare the final estimate line by line against the requirement register.
- For high-risk numbers (tonnage, total price), sample the calculation more than once and compare. Investigate any disagreement. Basis: self-consistency, Wang et al. 2022.
- Tag confidence high, medium, or low. Route low to a human.
- The model does not sign off on its own number. A human does.
