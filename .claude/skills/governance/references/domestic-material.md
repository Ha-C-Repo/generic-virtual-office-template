# Domestic-material guardrail (paste into procurement-packaging and line-item-pricing)

- BABA, 2 CFR Part 184. Iron and steel: all manufacturing from initial melting through coating in the United States. Re-melting imported billet does not qualify. Manufactured products: more than 55 percent domestic by component cost.
- FHWA manufactured-products waiver phase-out: US manufacture required from October 1, 2025; the 55 percent component-cost test applies from October 1, 2026.
- AISC 207-25 Domestic Fabricator certification. Per-address, annually audited, bid-determinative on many public projects.

Rule: any skill that touches material sourcing asserts domestic origin and flags BABA scope. On federally funded infrastructure the guardrail is mandatory.
